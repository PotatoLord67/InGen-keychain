# InGen-key chain
this project is a key chain of the in gen( international genetics) Logo from the jurasic park and Jurassic world movie/ TV show/ film franchise. it can be manufactured in almost any way, including 3d printable in different kinds of fillement including pla and tpu. it can also be manufactured on a CNS machiene

Description
for years, I have LOVED the Jurassic Park and Jurassic world franchises. I have also always wanted to get a key chain or something that shows my appreciation for the film series. I looked and looked, but everything was and is so expensive and overpriced. then I thought to myself: hey, I have onshape and I can make a key chain of my own practically for free, and I can make it look like whatever I want! so I started looking for inspiration pictures, and I found a decent quality one that I could check as I sketched the shapes.
